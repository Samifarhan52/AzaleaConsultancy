Work Permit & Skilled Employment Visas

A work visa refers to the immigration of a skilled person who has received job offers or qualifies under international points systems. Complete support for German Opportunity Cards (Chancenkarte), UK Skilled Worker Visas, Canada LMIA/Work Permits, and Gulf corporate placements.

  • Germany Chancenkarte: Points-based job-search visa with immediate European residency privileges.
  • UK Skilled Worker: Expedited employer-sponsored visa leading to Indefinite Leave to Remain (ILR).
  • Canada LMIA: Full legal handling for employer compliance and work authorization issuance.

Permanent Residency (PR) Programs

A Permanent Residency (PR) visa is a golden ticket for professionals looking to build enduring careers and family lives abroad. We structure petitions for Canada Express Entry (FSW/CEC), Provincial Nominee Programs (PNP), and Australia General Skilled Migration (Subclass 189/190/491).

  • Canada Express Entry: Comprehensive CRS score optimization, ECA audits, and provincial nominations.
  • Australia GSM: Skills assessment submission (Engineers Australia, ACS, VETASSESS) and state EOI filing.
  • Family Inclusion: Spouse open work permits and subsidized education for dependent children.

Germany Opportunity Card (Chancenkarte)

The Chancenkarte allows qualified professionals from non-EU nations to enter Germany for up to one year to secure qualified employment. Azalea calculates your 6 required points across qualifications, age, experience, and language proficiency.

  • Part-Time Work Permitted: Work up to 20 hours/week during your job search in Germany.
  • Direct EU Blue Card Pathway: Transition seamlessly into permanent residency upon receiving a qualified offer.
  • Expedited Embassy Booking: Priority appointment handling for Doha and Gulf residents.

Business & Golden Visa Programs

Immigrating through business investment is a mutually beneficial process. We provide strategic advisory for the UAE 10-Year Golden Visa, European real estate investment pathways (Portugal, Greece, Spain), and the US EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program.

  • European Golden Visas: Permanent residency with visa-free access across all 29 Schengen countries.
