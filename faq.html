Canada's Express Entry system scores candidates based on the Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS). Key scoring elements include age (optimal under 30), educational credentials evaluated via WES/ECA, continuous skilled work experience under TEER categories, and language test results (IELTS General or CELPIP with minimum CLB 7). Azalea conducts a complimentary CRS score optimization audit to identify provincial nominee (PNP) pathways that grant an automatic 600 bonus points.

The Chancenkarte (enacted in 2024) allows non-EU professionals to live in Germany for up to one year to seek qualified employment. You must achieve at least 6 points on a matrix covering recognized degree qualifications (4 pts), 5+ years experience (3 pts), German language A2 or English C1 (1-2 pts), and age under 35 (2 pts). Once you receive a qualifying job offer, Azalea transitions your status immediately into an EU Blue Card or permanent residency.
