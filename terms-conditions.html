General Terms of Engagement

These Terms & Conditions constitute a legally binding agreement between Azalea Tourism & Consultancy & Services (“Azalea”, “we”, “our”), registered under the commercial regulations of the State of Qatar, and the individual client or corporate entity (“Client”, “you”) engaging our visa evaluation, documentation, and relocation advisory services.

1 Scope of Professional Advisory Services

Azalea provides professional administrative assistance, profile eligibility scoring, consular document auditing, legal brief drafting, appointment scheduling, and procedural guidance for skilled work permits, permanent residency, corporate mobility, visit visas, and family reunification.

Our duties are strictly advisory, analytical, and representative within permitted administrative guidelines. We do not act as an embassy, consulate, or government visa-issuing department.

2 Sovereign Discretion & No Guarantee Disclaimer

Sovereign Prerogative Notice: The final decision to grant, refuse, or delay any visa, entry permit, or residence status rests solely with the competent sovereign government authorities, sovereign ministries, and consular officers of the destination nation. Azalea does not and cannot guarantee the issuance of any visa.

Our historical track record and approval metrics reflect meticulous document verification and adherence to statutory guidelines, but no advisor, employee, or agent of Azalea is authorized to promise an absolute guarantee of visa issuance.

3 Client Obligations & Document Authenticity

The Client expressly warrants that all documents, educational transcripts, employment records, bank statements, and personal declarations provided to Azalea are genuine, authentic, and accurate in all respects. Providing fraudulent, altered, or misleading documentation is strictly prohibited by law and will result in immediate termination of the retainer agreement without refund, and may incur statutory reporting under Qatari legal frameworks.

4 Fee Schedules, Invoicing & Retainers

Professional consulting retainers are invoiced in accordance with the specific milestone schedule detailed in your signed Retainer Agreement. All professional service fees are exclusive of official government consular fees, embassy visa filing fees, biometric fees, medical examination costs, third-party translations, and courier charges, which remain payable directly by the client or disbursed on an exact-cost pass-through basis.

5 Cancellation & Refund Policy

Due to the customized legal labor and profile analysis commenced immediately upon case onboarding, professional retainer fees are earned upon commencement of document auditing and file compilation:

  • Pre-Work Cancellation: If cancellation is requested in writing within 48 hours of retainer signing and prior to the commencement of case review, fees will be refunded less administrative onboarding costs.
  • Commenced Review: Once credential evaluation, document drafting, or petition compilation has begun, retainers are non-refundable.
  • Government Decisions: No refunds are provided for delays or adverse decisions resulting from sovereign policy changes, quota expirations, or consular discretion.

In all circumstances, our maximum aggregate liability is strictly limited to the actual professional fees paid by the client under the active engagement.

The parties irrevocably agree that the courts of Doha, Qatar shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or legal claim arising from our services.
